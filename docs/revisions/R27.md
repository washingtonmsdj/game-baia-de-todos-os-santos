# R27 — QA and Review Layer

## Scene state observed before this pass

The analyzed Blender file contained approximately:

- 4,617 objects;
- 4,097 meshes;
- 135 materials;
- 30 collections;
- 412 curves;
- 54 cameras;
- 1 explicit light;
- 270 mesh objects previously flagged with non-uniform scale/dimensions in internal audit data;
- 3,007 names with automatic Blender suffixes such as `.001`, `.002`, etc.;
- overall recorded bounds of roughly 1,892.30 × 1,471.74 × 129.82 meters.

## Existing scene/reference information

The file already recorded several important assumptions and historical passes:

- 944 OSM buildings were imported; buildings without known heights were blocked at 9 m and must not be treated as measured architecture.
- 311 OSM roads were recorded.
- Terrain source was recorded as Aleph DEM EPSG:3857 + OSM.
- The upper plaza area had been reconciled around ~70 m elevation; the lower exit around ~11 m; Mercado Modelo around ~10.9 m.
- The slope profile is functional for the MVP but is not a final road survey.
- Praça Castro Alves / Ladeira da Conceição contain omitted road fragments caused by DEM cliff artifacts.
- The upper entrance was opened but its final footprint still requires stronger reference confirmation.
- Cabin/door/threshold alignments were already adjusted to recorded elevations.
- The elevator shaft had recorded dimensions around 3.22 × 6.60 m with a large vertical clear range.
- The lower exit had been opened, but local terrain/elevation remained an MVP approximation.
- Later revisions added additional façade, building, terrain-edge, plaza, and access work through R26.

## Why R27 exists

The scene was no longer an early blockout. The main immediate risk was maintainability: thousands of objects, many automatically suffixed names, many cameras, and weak separation between reference geometry, gameplay geometry, and presentation helpers.

Mass renaming was intentionally avoided because Blender object names can be referenced by constraints, drivers, scripts, exporters, or manual workflows.

## R27 changes

`tools/blender/r27_qa_review.py` performs a non-destructive continuation pass:

1. creates `30 MVP | QA E GUIAS R27`;
2. adds QA markers for the upper entrance, walkway, cabins, lower exit, Praça Cairu, and Mercado Modelo;
3. adds a review camera;
4. creates `31 LUZ | PREVIEW R27` with optional preview lights that are disabled for render by default;
5. writes `R27_README` inside Blender;
6. records R27 checkpoint metadata on the Scene;
7. saves a new `_r27.blend` instead of overwriting the source.

## Outcome

R27 improves reviewability and establishes safe spatial checkpoints without changing landmark architecture or performing destructive cleanup.
